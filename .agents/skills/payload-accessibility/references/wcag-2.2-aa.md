# WCAG 2.2 Level AA engineering reference

WCAG 2.2 Level AA requires every applicable Level A and Level AA success criterion. The summaries below are engineering prompts, not replacements for the normative [WCAG 2.2 Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) or its [How to Meet WCAG quick reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/).

For a code change, assess every criterion the change can plausibly affect. Record applicability, tested states, evidence, and anything requiring human review. Do not mark a criterion `Not Applicable` merely because the current test fixture lacks the relevant content.

## Conformance boundary

Automated tests establish only the behaviors they assert. Product-level VPAT/ACR work must also define the evaluated product scope and environments, cover full pages and complete processes, assess every applicable criterion, include manual and assistive-technology evidence, account for known defects, and receive qualified human review. Axe results, passing regressions, or closed issues cannot independently establish `Supports`.
